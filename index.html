About Mufti Services Limited

Technology • Innovation • Skills Development

Mufti Services Limited is a Nigerian technology company committed to delivering innovative digital solutions that empower individuals, businesses, government institutions and non-governmental organizations.

We specialize in Information Technology, Digital Marketing, Software Development, ICT Consultancy, Business Solutions, Financial Technology and Youth Empowerment Programmes.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between technology and society through innovation, capacity building and sustainable digital transformation.
